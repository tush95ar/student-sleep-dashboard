Methodology & data notes

Data source

  • 500 students, 25 fields covering sleep, screen use, lifestyle, study and attendance, and cumulative GPA (CGPA, 4.0 scale): student_sleep_screen_time_academic_performance.csv.
  • Students are identified only by pseudonymous IDs. The data is a single cross-sectional snapshot, with no time dimension.

Cleaning (clean_data.py; no rows dropped)

  • Gender: 105 inconsistent labels normalised (M / male → Male, F / female → Female).
  • Bedtime: times after midnight shifted +24 h so they sort after 23:00. Correct median 23:42; a naive average would read 12:47.
  • Missing values are kept and flagged, never imputed. GPA metrics exclude the 7 students without CGPA. Sleep quality (6), energy (15), concentration (14) and device-free routine (9) are excluded only from the metrics that use them. Every tile shows its own n.

Outliers (flagged, not removed)

  • 27 students (5.4%): weekday sleep < 4 h or > 10 h, daily screen > 14 h, study > 60 h/week, or attendance < 50%. Use Exclude outliers to drop them. Results barely change (sleep β 0.41 → 0.40).

  • v2 replaced caffeine with low study hours, because caffeine showed no effect once sleep was controlled. Correlation with CGPA strengthened from r = −0.47 to −0.56.

Statistics used

  • Drivers chart: Pearson r with CGPA on all 493 students with CGPA, plus standardised β from multiple regression. It does not change with filters.
  • Sleep-adjusted chronotype: group means of CGPA evaluated at the average sleep of shown students, using a pooled within-group slope (ANCOVA).
  • What-if slider: simple linear slopes of sleep, sleep quality and CGPA on pre-sleep screen minutes (all students), applied to each shown student's own reduction. The ± range is the 95% interval of the slope.
  • Dot charts show means with 95% confidence intervals (1.96 × SE).

Display rules

  • Deltas on KPI tiles compare the current view with all 500 students. Targets are proposals pending owner sign-off.
  • Groups under 30 students are faded and badged “low n”. Heatmap cells under 10 are hidden (hatched).
  • Profile actions are rule-based suggestions with thresholds pending owner sign-off. They are not diagnoses.

Validation

  • Every KPI was recomputed independently in Python (validate_dashboard.py) for no filters and 3 random filter combinations. All 368 value, n and displayed-text checks matched. Details: validation_report.md.

Caveats

  • Associations, not causes. The data is observational and self-reported. Sleep, study and attendance together explain about 28–34% of CGPA variance; most variation is unexplained.
  • Seven majors and several subgroups are small. Treat differences within small groups as hypotheses.
